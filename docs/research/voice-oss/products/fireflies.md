# Fireflies: documented product and flow inventory

Research date: **2026-10-12**. This addition contributes **30 flows and 38 sources** to the [flow catalogue](../data/flows.json) and [source registry](../data/sources.jsonl). It is a reference for broad meeting workflows and composition; it is not an endorsement of cloud storage or a transcription-quality ranking.

**Evidence boundary:** all flows are DOCUMENTED first-party behavior. No app, account, browser extension, binary, API key, meeting, or network implementation was exercised. UX quality, latency, accuracy, accessibility, privacy controls and failure durability remain untested. Analysis and proposed lessons are marked INFERRED; absence of evidence is UNKNOWN.

Fireflies combines a meeting bot, desktop device-audio capture, Meet Chrome extension, mobile recording, file uploads, a cloud meeting library, AskFred, and desktop dictation. Those surfaces have materially different storage and speaker guarantees. **Desktop Take Notes does not save audio/video and supplies generic labels; this is not the same as the Meet extension or mobile recorder.**

## Flow catalogue

**FF-F01 — Schedule selective bot capture** (Web + connected Google/Outlook calendar). Connect calendar; choose auto-join scope or enable one upcoming event; apply inclusion/exclusion rules. **Result:** Bot attempts eligible scheduled meetings. **Branch/limit:** Conferencing link required; adding an email invite after start is insufficient; bot leaves an empty meeting after ten minutes. [FF-S03](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4335268657)

**FF-F02 — Invite a bot to a live meeting** (Web / desktop). Open Capture or Add Notetaker; enter meeting URL and language; start; admit the bot if required. **Result:** Participant bot supplies a meeting record. **Branch/limit:** Join delay and host policy can block capture; named-speaker/media features depend on this mode. [FF-S01](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1208704416-getting-started-with-the-fireflies-desktop-app), [FF-S03](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4335268657)

**FF-F03 — Capture from desktop device audio** (macOS / Windows). Click Take Notes, or choose it from a detected-call prompt; use floating live pane; finish. **Result:** Transcript and summary appear in the library. **Branch/limit:** No saved audio/video and only generic speaker labels; switching to bot requires a separate session. [FF-S02](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6666374717-how-to-record-meetings-without-a-bot-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app)

**FF-F04 — Disclose desktop capture** (Desktop; macOS chat support). At first use/update or in Recording settings select chat/audio notices; optionally choose Fireflies Watermark camera. **Result:** Participants can receive a capture signal. **Branch/limit:** Zoom/Meet chat posting uses Accessibility; audio notice depends on speaker/mic routing; signal delivery is untested. [FF-S04](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3917896272-how-consent-notifications-work-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app)

**FF-F05 — Capture Meet from Chrome** (Google Meet in Chrome). Install/sign in/authorize extension; choose auto-capture or start manually; stop and open Notebook. **Result:** Meeting audio and notes are saved without a participant bot. **Branch/limit:** Extension capture is Meet-only and audio-only; video needs bot; no desktop-mode retention assumptions. [FF-S05](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/8146815126-learn-about-fireflies-chrome-extension)

**FF-F06 — Use live transcript and assistance** (Web / desktop / extension / mobile). Open Live Assist; inspect transcript/AI notes/prep; request catch-up, actions or a contextual answer; share according to access. **Result:** Live meeting context remains available during conversation. **Branch/limit:** Bot requirement varies by surface; named labels are mode-specific; dynamic prompts/queries may consume credits. [FF-S06](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7077919424-explore-the-real-time-pane-on-the-fireflies-chrome-extension), [FF-S07](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6032274417-learn-about-fireflies-live-assist-get-real-time-suggestions-answers-and-notes-live-during-the-meeting)

**FF-F07 — Write private notes and merge deliberately** (Desktop live pane). Switch to Manual Notes; type; leave private or enable Merge to summary; revisit after capture. **Result:** Personal notes autosave; selected content informs shared output. **Branch/limit:** Merged notes become visible to recap viewers; no immutable version history established. [FF-S08](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4961444690-manual-notes-on-fireflies-desktop-app-write-and-merge-your-own-notes-in-meetings)

**FF-F08 — Capture a relayed phone call** (Mac with iPhone / Windows with Phone Link). Enable phone relay to computer; make or receive call through that route; use desktop Take Notes. **Result:** Call audio produces meeting text and summary. **Branch/limit:** No saved media in this mode; does not establish iOS-app PSTN origination or native phone-call interception. [FF-S02](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6666374717-how-to-record-meetings-without-a-bot-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app)

**FF-F09 — Record an in-person mobile conversation** (iOS / Android). Tap Record; add title or skip; pause/resume; stop; inspect status and Notebook. **Result:** Phone-microphone audio becomes a meeting. **Branch/limit:** No other-app call audio or Watch capture established; processing requires network. [FF-S19](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4565146696-how-to-transcribe-offline-meetings-using-fireflies-mobile-app)

**FF-F10 — Recover an offline mobile recording** (iOS / Android). Record offline; open Profile > Local Recordings; reconnect; choose Upload, title and language; track status. **Result:** Queued audio reaches transcription when online. **Branch/limit:** No device-loss protection or documented indefinite post-upload device retention. [FF-S20](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1360888790-how-to-upload-unprocessed-files-in-the-fireflies-mobile-app)

**FF-F11 — Import existing audio or video** (Web). Upload supported file; set meeting information/language; monitor processing; open Uploads or timeline. **Result:** Existing source produces transcript and insights. **Branch/limit:** 150-minute duration limit; format/size quotas apply; no speech yields no transcript and short files may lack recaps. [FF-S21](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3893959957-how-to-upload-and-transcribe-audio-or-video-files-in-fireflies)

**FF-F12 — Review, edit and clip evidence** (Meeting Notepad). Open meeting; compare summary and transcript; search/edit text; play available media; select text to create a soundbite. **Result:** Evidence and derived notes can be checked and shared. **Branch/limit:** Playback needs retained media; undo/redo does not establish durable version history. [FF-S09](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6653885315-learn-about-the-fireflies-notepad)

**FF-F13 — Correct speaker identities** (Meeting owner in Notepad). Rename one occurrence or all matching labels; split mixed-speaker paragraphs; save and regenerate notes. **Result:** Transcript and subsequent summary use corrected identities. **Branch/limit:** Source audio stays unchanged; overlap and initial identity mechanism remain unresolved. [FF-S10](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4994477228-how-to-edit-speaker-labels-or-names-in-a-transcript)

**FF-F14 — Find and organize meeting history** (Web / desktop library). Search/filter by participant/date/capture source; choose channel; move or remove meetings; manage channel membership. **Result:** Related meetings form a reusable context set. **Branch/limit:** Removing/deleting a channel preserves meetings; deleting a meeting is different and permanent. [FF-S31](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4827382971-learn-about-fireflies-notebook), [FF-S32](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7309931227-how-to-delete-a-channel)

**FF-F15 — Ask about one processed meeting** (Meeting Notepad). Open processed meeting; select AskFred; ask a question and follow up. **Result:** Answer uses that meeting context. **Branch/limit:** Paid-meeting chat credit behavior differs from live/global chat; current guide removes model picker here. [FF-S12](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6556345325-askfred-ask-fred-questions-from-your-meetings-in-fireflies-and-get-answers)

**FF-F16 — Ask across history and added context** (Global AskFred). Choose meeting/person/group context; attach files or dictate/edit prompt; select offered model; submit. **Result:** Cross-meeting synthesis uses selected context. **Branch/limit:** Model/default names drift within docs; hosted model menu does not establish BYOK or local inference. [FF-S11](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1512776728-learn-about-global-askfred)

**FF-F17 — Create reusable AI processing** (AI Skills). Choose template or create custom instructions; enable skill; inspect Active Skills and output Feed. **Result:** Repeated meetings generate structured outputs. **Branch/limit:** Role controls and AI credit costs apply; not evidence of local hooks or portable skills files. [FF-S33](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6161594443-learn-about-ai-skills-create-automated-workflows-from-your-meetings)

**FF-F18 — Connect tools and act from AskFred** (AskFred MCP client). Add remote server; choose OAuth/API authentication; authorize; enable permitted tools; request an external action. **Result:** Meeting context can create or update downstream work. **Branch/limit:** Connector authorization and permissions are documented; universal per-action confirmation, undo and idempotency are not. [FF-S13](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7673197830-connect-a-custom-mcp-server-to-askfred)

**FF-F19 — Use meetings through external MCP clients** (External AI client). Configure Fireflies hosted MCP; authenticate; search/fetch authorized meetings or invoke available actions. **Result:** Other agents consume meeting context and can perform supported writes. **Branch/limit:** Not read-only: catalogue includes sharing and soundbite creation; some search tools are experimental. [FF-S14](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3039542843-learn-about-fireflies-mcp-server-connect-your-ai-tool), [FF-S15](https://docs.fireflies.ai/mcp-tools/overview), [FF-S36](https://docs.fireflies.ai/getting-started/mcp-configuration)

**FF-F20 — Build an API integration** (Developer client). Obtain API key; submit GraphQL queries; for live use discover meeting ID and subscribe to realtime updates. **Result:** Structured meeting records or timed transcript segments feed another tool. **Branch/limit:** No live request tested; repeated chunk IDs indicate updates; output speaker names do not reveal attribution mechanism. [FF-S16](https://docs.fireflies.ai/getting-started/quickstart), [FF-S18](https://docs.fireflies.ai/realtime-api/getting-started), [FF-S35](https://docs.fireflies.ai/realtime-api/event-schema)

**FF-F21 — React to meeting lifecycle webhooks** (Developer HTTPS receiver). Configure V2 URL, selected events and signing secret; verify incoming signature; fetch ready transcript/summary. **Result:** Bot-joined/transcribed/summarized events trigger workflows. **Branch/limit:** Owned-meeting scope; finite transient retries; a connected receiver response timeout is not retried; team scope is Enterprise/Super Admin. [FF-S17](https://docs.fireflies.ai/graphql-api/webhooks-v2)

**FF-F22 — Set up and dictate into other apps** (Mac / Windows desktop Talk). Choose language/mic/shortcut; grant permissions; practice; hold shortcut or double-tap; speak and finish in target field. **Result:** Formatted text enters the active app. **Branch/limit:** Mac needs Accessibility; mobile Talk is coming soon; formatting quality untested. [FF-S27](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7997164159-how-to-set-up-fireflies-talk-on-mac), [FF-S28](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3809925819-how-to-use-fireflies-talk-to-dictate-in-any-app), [FF-S38](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1890620054-is-fireflies-talk-free-plans-and-availability)

**FF-F23 — Recover failed text insertion** (Desktop Talk). When insertion fails, paste clipboard text or copy Not pasted entry from Dictation history; repair shortcut/mic selection. **Result:** Recognized text remains recoverable for another paste. **Branch/limit:** Clipboard/history is text recovery, not retained original audio; AirPods and Fn conflicts are documented. [FF-S28](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3809925819-how-to-use-fireflies-talk-to-dictate-in-any-app), [FF-S30](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6517448179-fireflies-talk-troubleshooting-text-not-appearing-wrong-words-shortcut-not-working)

**FF-F24 — Control dictation context and history** (Desktop Talk). Toggle optional Screen context; copy/delete individual history or clear it from settings; revoke OS permissions when needed. **Result:** User controls contextual processing and local text history. **Branch/limit:** Context sends audio/screen information to vendor/providers; raw dictation audio is discarded after processing. [FF-S29](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/8264684551-fireflies-talk-privacy-where-your-dictations-go-and-who-can-see-them)

**FF-F25 — Reprocess a meeting language** (Meeting owner / host). Open language selector; choose language; confirm Reprocess; refresh; regenerate summary separately if stale. **Result:** A replacement transcript/summary is produced. **Branch/limit:** Maximum three retranscriptions; existing manual transcript edits are lost; no-bot session eligibility unspecified. [FF-S23](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1881999022-how-to-re-transcribe-a-meeting-into-a-different-language)

**FF-F26 — Export text and media** (Pro or higher). Open processed meeting Download; choose transcript/summary/media and format/timestamps/speakers; save. **Result:** Portable artifacts leave the vendor library. **Branch/limit:** Media options exist only when captured; export is not automatic local retention. [FF-S22](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3319752033-how-to-download-transcripts-summaries-and-meeting-recordings-from-fireflies)

**FF-F27 — Delete owned meetings** (Owner; bulk Pro+). Select meeting(s) or Notepad menu; choose Delete and confirm. **Result:** Meeting and associated stored content are removed. **Branch/limit:** Documented as permanent with no restore; external downloaded/shared copies are outside this flow. [FF-S24](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1311785862-how-to-delete-a-meeting-from-fireflies)

**FF-F28 — Set future-meeting retention** (Enterprise user / admin). Enable auto-delete; choose interval; admin may enforce team choice. **Result:** Future meetings expire under the owner/team policy. **Branch/limit:** Focused guide says not retroactive; policy can remove originals and outputs. [FF-S25](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7870593373-learn-about-the-auto-delete-meeting-feature)

**FF-F29 — Select private storage or BYOS** (Enterprise). Configure private storage with vendor; enable for new meetings; coordinate BYOS/location changes. **Result:** Meeting data is stored in the configured private bucket. **Branch/limit:** Processing remains on Fireflies servers in the US; location/setup wording varies; not full self-hosting. [FF-S26](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3687416644-learn-about-private-storage)

**FF-F30 — Automate routing and access policy** (Enterprise admin). Create rule; combine title/host/participant/type filters; choose route/share/privacy/retention/language actions; test and inspect logs. **Result:** Matching meetings receive consistent downstream policy. **Branch/limit:** These are cloud policies, not XDG dotfiles; rollback/exactly-once execution unknown. [FF-S34](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/4608292950-learn-about-the-rules-engine-feature)

## Ownership, history and models

Mobile offline capture is a local queue awaiting cloud processing; this does not prove that originals remain on-device after successful upload. Talk stores text/app/time locally but discards original dictation audio. Its optional screen-context mode sends audio/context to Fireflies and providers. These are distinct lifecycles, not a single local-first data model. [FF-S20](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1360888790-how-to-upload-unprocessed-files-in-the-fireflies-mobile-app), [FF-S29](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/8264684551-fireflies-talk-privacy-where-your-dictations-go-and-who-can-see-them)

Enterprise Private Storage/BYOS is a storage option: the guide explicitly keeps processing on Fireflies servers in the US. Global AskFred offers a vendor-managed model menu; no user-hosted inference endpoint or BYOK flow is established. The selected public API and MCP endpoints belong to Fireflies. No OSS license, complete self-hosting distribution, XDG configuration, native terminal CLI, iCloud sync, Apple Watch capture, or iOS custom keyboard is established in this pass. [FF-S11](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1512776728-learn-about-global-askfred), [FF-S26](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3687416644-learn-about-private-storage), [FF-S36](https://docs.fireflies.ai/getting-started/mcp-configuration), [FF-S38](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1890620054-is-fireflies-talk-free-plans-and-availability)

Reprocessing replaces transcript edits and is capped; deletion has no restore. These directly conflict with retaining every original and derived version. **INFERRED lesson:** keep recording evidence, speaker observations, corrected identity maps, and regenerated outputs as independently versioned objects rather than overwriting the only transcript. [FF-S23](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1881999022-how-to-re-transcribe-a-meeting-into-a-different-language), [FF-S24](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1311785862-how-to-delete-a-meeting-from-fireflies)

## Composability and action controls

Distinguish **MCP server** (external clients reach Fireflies) from **MCP client** (AskFred reaches other services). The former includes documented write tools, not just search. The latter exposes authorization and connector permissions; a universal review-before-write step is **UNKNOWN**, so it is not credited as a verified approval design. No connected tool was authorized or invoked. [FF-S13](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7673197830-connect-a-custom-mcp-server-to-askfred), [FF-S15](https://docs.fireflies.ai/mcp-tools/overview)

Webhooks V2 separate bot arrival, transcript readiness and summary readiness. Signing is optional unless configured. Delivery has bounded retries and explicitly does not retry a connected receiver that fails to answer before its response deadline. **INFERRED lesson:** our hooks need durable local events, observable status, idempotency, replay, and source-version IDs; a webhook checkbox alone is insufficient. [FF-S17](https://docs.fireflies.ai/graphql-api/webhooks-v2)

## Fit for the requested product

**INFERRED strengths to study:** integrated before/during/after meeting surfaces; human notes beside AI output; explicit source-type filters; speaker correction at segment and identity levels; text-insertion recovery; external-agent access and tools within the app. Fireflies complements the expansion's local-model and personal-knowledge references by exposing mature cloud workflow breadth. This is design analysis, not proof that its visual design meets native Apple standards.

**INFERRED gaps relative to the brief:** retained raw audio across every mode, non-destructive processing history, user-controlled hosting/inference, native Apple platform cohesion, portable local hooks/configuration, and Watch/keyboard/iCloud coverage. Its documented desktop phone relay is useful to study but does not satisfy the requirement to originate ordinary-number calls from the iOS app.
