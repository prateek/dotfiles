# Wholesale, imports and delivery

Read for supplier sourcing, cross-border orders, or location/time-dependent retail,
grocery and prepared-food offers. These branches need different evidence.

## Supplier and import sourcing

Separate a one-unit consumer purchase from samples, wholesale and manufacturing.
AliExpress may suit small-quantity comparison; Alibaba, 1688, Global Sources and
specialist distributors may suit sourcing when quantity and import logistics fit.
Discover regional alternatives and actual manufacturers; a platform name does
not prove seller identity, certification or local fulfillment.

Inspect exact specification, variant, MOQ, price tiers, sample terms, production
lead time, shipping basis and destination eligibility. Distinguish an indicative
price from a seller quotation and a trade term from a delivered total. Include
known shipping, tax/duty, brokerage and local handling costs; state uncertain ones.
Use an authoritative current source for jurisdiction-specific rules when material.

For electronics, compare authorized component distributors and specialist retailers
alongside marketplaces when provenance, warranty or counterfeit risk matters.
Preserve manufacturer part numbers. Certifications and compatibility claims need
supporting evidence appropriate to the decision. Finish with comparable quantities
and terms, or explain why offers cannot yet be compared. Research grants no
permission to contact suppliers, request quotes containing private data or order.

## Address-dependent retail and delivery

Examples include Instacart, Uber Eats, DoorDash, regional grocery services and
retailers' own delivery sites. Use the actual market and an authorized service
context. A generic landing page is not an inventory result.

Check store/branch, service area, item or menu variant, stock, minimum basket,
mandatory fees, taxes, substitutions and delivery/pickup time. Separate membership
or promotional prices from generally available prices. Treat a changed address,
store or slot as a different offer context. Keep private addresses and account
details in the authorized session, outside requests, reusable preferences and
evaluation artifacts; public city/region context is usually sufficient for discovery.

When service context is unavailable, compare public information and label current
availability/total cost unknown. Stop on login/security gates and report gaps.
Finish with an observed offer in the relevant context or an explicitly limited
comparison. Cart additions, reservations and checkout require separate authority.
