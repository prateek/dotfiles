# East Asian source seeds

Read for Chinese-, Japanese- or Korean-language evidence. These examples identify
possible audiences and evidence; they are not a capability inventory. Check the
specific community, public visibility and current route before claiming coverage.

| Context | Useful starting sources | Decision checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mainland Chinese public discussion | Weibo; Zhihu for explanatory discussion; Tieba or a specialist forum for niche communities | Find the original post and replies; distinguish experience from reposts, marketing and unsourced answers |
| Chinese developer experience | V2EX, Juejin, project issues and Gitee; specialist engineering forums | Verify versions, runnable code and project provenance; a mirror is not necessarily the upstream |
| Chinese demonstrations and owner reports | Bilibili, Douyin, Rednote/Xiaohongshu | Inspect the relevant spoken/visual segment; captions, lifestyle posts and sponsorship are different evidence |
| Traditional Chinese communities | PTT, Dcard, Mobile01; LIHKG for relevant Hong Kong discussions | Use the appropriate script, locality and forum section; Taiwan and Hong Kong are distinct markets |
| Japanese technical practice | Qiita, Zenn, Hatena, note, project-host discussions | Check article dates, revisions and whether a personal example establishes the requested behavior |
| Japanese public/owner discussion | X, specialist forums and relevant 5ch threads; demonstrations on video platforms | Identify original context and model variants; anonymous anecdotes need corroboration |
| Korean technical discussion | GeekNews (`news.hada.io`), project-host discussions, Naver/Daum communities | Search Korean terms plus exact identifiers; inspect the original linked discussion and follow-up |
| Korean consumer experience | Relevant Naver cafes/blogs, Clien, DCInside sections; local video reports | Check community fit, affiliate incentives, product variant and accessible replies |

Use simplified/traditional script variants only when the task spans those audiences.
Japanese and Korean searches often mix local terms with Latin product identifiers.
Resolve ambiguous names against manufacturers or project documentation. Avoid
assuming the model can confidently interpret every idiom or specialized term.

For offers, discover the relevant local market separately: Taobao/JD/Pinduoduo or
1688 in mainland China, Rakuten/Yahoo! Auctions/Mercari in Japan, Coupang/Naver
Shopping/Karrot in Korea, and the appropriate local retailers elsewhere. Wholesale,
consumer retail and local resale have different quantities and fulfillment terms.
Read the shopping guide for evidence and the specialist branch for used or imports.

When a page requires access you lack, retain it as an uninspected candidate and
seek accessible alternatives. A searchable title is not full community coverage.
Finish with the communities and languages actually inspected, plus material gaps.
